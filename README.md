## CTF Reflection 🧠

The NCL competition helped me strengthen my technical workflow and confidence. I practiced slowing down, identifying the artifact type, choosing the right tool, documenting what I tried, and validating results before submitting an answer.

Some challenges required manual analysis, while others involved packet captures, logs, browser developer tools, scripting, cryptography, password cracking, and forensic investigation. I also learned that failed attempts are not wasted time. Sometimes an error message is just the challenge politely whispering, “wrong door, try again.”

The Team Game especially reinforced the value of collaboration. Having teammates to compare results with, divide categories, and confirm findings helped us move faster and avoid tunnel vision.
